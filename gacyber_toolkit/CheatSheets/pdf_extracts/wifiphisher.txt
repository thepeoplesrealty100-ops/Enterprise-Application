Wifiphisher
Source: https://wifiphisher.org
Wifiphisher is a tool that can automate the phishing attacks against Wi-Fi networks. It can be
used to obtain WPA/WPA2 secret passphrases without performing brute force.
Syntax
wifiphisher [Options]
